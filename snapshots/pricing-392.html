Before you pay

The terms, in full.

Not a link to a policy page. This is all of it.

It renews by itself
Whichever plan you pick renews automatically, at the price you signed up at, until you cancel it. The annual plans renew once a year on the day you started; month to month renews on the same date every month.
What you will be charged
$400 a year, $40 a month, or $50 a month — whichever one you chose above — plus sales tax in the states that charge it. The price does not change on renewal.
Only one plan is a commitment
Annual paid monthly means 12 months. The other two have no term of any kind: the up-front year is already paid for, and month to month ends whenever you say so.
Leaving that one early
You pay half of the months still to run, charged that day to the card on file. On 12 months at $40 a month, walking away halfway through costs $120. Nothing else, and nothing after it.
How you cancel
One button in your own account, any time, in the same place you signed up. No phone call, no email and nobody to talk out of it. You keep everything you have already paid for until that period ends.
The build fee is separate
The $499 to build the site is charged once, at the start, and is not part of the subscription. It stops being refundable once work on your site begins, and you are told on the day that happens.
If a payment fails
We try the card again over the following two weeks and email you each time. If it still has not gone through after that, the site goes offline until it is paid. Nothing is deleted and it comes straight back.
When you leave
Cancelling stops the next charge. Your customers, their numbers and your photos are yours — you export them and go. The domain is in your name and leaves with you.

Every plan runs the same software. The only thing that changes is how long you commit for, and what that saves you.

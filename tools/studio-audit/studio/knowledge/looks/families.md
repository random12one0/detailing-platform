# The style families (v1)

Each is a whole look: fonts, colours, corners, first-screen layout and section rhythm are drawn by the renderer.
Write the words to suit it. Keep the work (proof) second in every order: a customer should see it within two phone
screens. The orders differ after that.

## showroom: Showroom
- Look: near-black ground, bone type, copper for the one thing to press. One big photo fills the first screen and
  the headline is set huge, in condensed capitals, across its foot (the "overlay" layout).
- Fonts: Big Shoulders Display (condensed, set in capitals) with Figtree.
- Suggested order: hero, proof, catalog, visit, booking (photos lead: the work sells it).
- Voice: bold and short. Headlines of three to six words read best in capitals; a two-sentence headline puts the
  payoff in copper. Section titles short and declarative ("The work", "The menu").
- Headlines that suit it: "Clean inside. Sharp outside.", "Done in your driveway.", "The work speaks first."
- Avoid: long headlines (they run to many lines in capitals), exclamation marks, "showroom shine".

## neighbourhood: Neighbourhood
- Look: sky-blue ground, navy type, sunflower yellow; rounded white cards; the words come first and the photo
  sits below like a snapshot (the "stack" layout). The area served is a pill above the headline.
- Fonts: Changa One (round, friendly) with Figtree.
- Suggested order: hero, proof, catalog, booking, visit.
- Voice: warm, first person plural, neighbourly ("We come to you"). A two-sentence headline gets the second
  sentence highlighted in yellow: make it the friendly payoff.
- Headlines that suit it: "Clean car, no trip across town.", "Your car, done while you get on with your day."
- Say how the detailer works ONCE: the area pill already says where; do not repeat "we come to you" in both the
  headline and the line under it. Use the line under it for a different, true benefit the dashboard supports.
- Avoid: cute puns, "family-owned" (unless told), anything that sounds like an ad.

## workshop: Precision workshop
- Look: porcelain white with a faint grid, graphite type, cobalt rules; square corners; every price is on the first
  screen as a spec sheet and the photo is small (the "ledger" layout). Facts first.
- Fonts: Barlow Condensed (set in capitals) with Source Sans 3.
- Suggested order: hero, proof, booking, catalog, visit (the first screen is already the price list; then the work, then booking).
- Voice: terse, exact, mechanical. Say what is done and how prices are worked out. Titles like labels
  ("Prices by vehicle size", "Hours and area").
- Headlines that suit it: "Priced by vehicle size. Checked before you book.", "What it costs, what is included."
- Avoid: warmth for its own sake, adjectives, anything vague.

## boutique: Boutique
- Look: cool white, petrol ink, a soft high-contrast serif; a tall framed photo beside the words like a magazine
  page (the "split" layout); generous space.
- Fonts: Fraunces (serif, the second sentence of a headline in italic) with Public Sans.
- Suggested order: hero, proof, visit, catalog, booking (where and when before the prices, like a calm shop window).
- Voice: quiet and exact; calm confidence; short sentences with room around them. A two-sentence headline sets the
  second sentence in italic.
- Headlines that suit it: "Careful work. Clear prices.", "Your car, looked after properly."
- Avoid: luxury clichés ("bespoke", "curated", "indulge", "elevate"), anything that sounds expensive for its own sake.

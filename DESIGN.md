VISUAL REDESIGN IN PROGRESS (owner decision, 2026-08-28).

The previous design system, docs/design-system.md ("Raking Light"), is no
longer the identity. It was chosen from a handful of options before the
owner's design research existed, and the owner has called for a complete
visual restart. Until a new direction is chosen and documented:

- Treat docs/design-system.md as EVIDENCE of the old look, not a contract.
  Do not apply it to new work; do not polish toward it.
- Backend behavior, content, copy facts, accessibility floors, and the
  booking engine are all KEPT. Only the visual world is being replaced.
- The new direction gets chosen in the redesign phase of docs/roadmap.md,
  then documented in a rewritten docs/design-system.md, and the design
  tests in tests/ get rewritten to enforce the new rules. After that, the
  new system is law the way the old one was.

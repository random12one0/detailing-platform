# Business rules — read before every job

Andrew's standing rules for how the business and the product work. **Every job reads this
file before it starts** (the belt puts it in every brief). A rule here outranks an older
roadmap entry, a test, or a document that says otherwise; if you find a conflict, follow
the rule and write `NEEDS ANDREW:` naming the conflict.

"Decided" means Andrew decided it. "Not built yet" means no job has built it — do not build
it unless your item asks for it, but never build anything that contradicts it.

Planning adds to this file automatically when Andrew settles a conversation (its RULES
section). Edit by hand only to add a rule Andrew stated, with the date.

## Messaging

- **Email is the record, text is the nudge.** (Decided 2026-10-10, Planning → roadmap 99.2.)
  Customers get short texts about their booking only if they ticked the one unticked consent
  box under the phone field; everyone else gets email. Receipts, invoices and all marketing
  go by email only. Marketing texts stay off.
- Every customer message uses the plain, warm wording approved in Planning; detailers can add
  a personal note but cannot rewrite our wording or the legal parts. (Decided 2026-10-10.)

## Bookings — decided 2026-10-12, not built yet

Source: Andrew's email-by-email feedback in Planning ("Every customer email, one by one");
full worked detail in `docs/planning/email-copy-and-rules-draft-2026-10-12.md` §§ A–F.

- **Customers cannot move their own date instantly.** Changing the date sends a request the
  detailer approves or declines — even when the business normally accepts bookings
  automatically. Only one move request can be pending per booking; the original appointment
  stays booked until approved. Fixing contact details, notes or an address typo stays easy and
  direct.
- **Detailers can turn on a cancellation fee, and optionally a rescheduling fee.** Off by
  default, shown to the customer before they book. Cancellation emails must not say "Nothing
  is owed".
- **Maintenance plans:** detailers can require a minimum number of details and/or charge an
  early-cancellation fee, and can edit or cancel a plan on a customer's behalf. Plan
  notifications name the car(s) on the plan.
- **"Stop reminding me"** on maintenance reminders is a button to a page (optional note to the
  detailer), never "reply to this email".

## Platform billing — decided 2026-10-12, not built yet

- **If a detailer's card keeps failing for about two weeks, their account goes on lockdown:**
  they can finish existing bookings and collect money for them, but cannot take, create or
  accept new bookings, and everything else is locked except paying, logging in and their
  records. If the platform hosts their premium main website, it is replaced with a neutral
  "unavailable" page (not deleted, and the billing problem is never shown publicly).

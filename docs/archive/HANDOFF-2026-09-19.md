## The decision Andrew made, and what it cost

**ROADMAP 8.4 replaced a small eye icon with the words "What's included".**
He asked for it; two audits asked for it. The code had defended the icon ON
HEIGHT, and it was right to: a worded control takes a row of its own.

**Then the repaired booking sweep priced that row: 264px on a six-service step,
which turned step 1 from fitting at three widths into overflowing at three.**

**His decision, 2026-09-19: shrink the paint, keep the 44px target.** Done.
The row paints **27px**; the target is **45px**, held by an absolutely
positioned `::after` — `docs/screen-rules.md`'s own pattern.

| step 1 | pre-8.4 | after 8.4 | after the decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1920 | fits, 133 spare | over 131 | **fits, 25 spare** |
| 1440 | fits, 10 spare | over 254 | over 98 |
| 768 | fits, 166 spare | over 98 | **fits, 58 spare** |
| 392 | fits, 46 spare | over 218 | over 62 |

**The residual is exactly 108px at both remaining widths** — six cards × 18px.
That is the floor of this trade, not a tuning failure: closing it means taking
the words off a row of their own, which is the thing that was asked for.

**Do not "fix" this by shrinking the target.** It is 45px and it TILES with its
neighbours — 9px up (paid for by `.bk-svc .bk-pick` giving up 9px of padding)
and 8px down (into the 8px card gap). Probed pixel by pixel: below the paint,
+4..+8 belong to the control and +9 onward to the next card. A symmetric −8px
was tried first and measured 42px, under the floor. **A bounding rect cannot
see any of this** — use `elementFromPoint`.

## The W16 question — asked and answered

**Add-ons overflows by 476px at 392 — 56% of the screen — and it is a decision,
not a defect.** The sweep names `.bk-planpitch` (472px). That is the
monthly-plan pitch, and the code quotes him three times: *"pretty big and
pretty visible… like, just in the whole screen, like, not a button to go to the
screen, but just the whole screen."*

**ANSWERED 2026-09-19 — nothing is waiting on him any more.**

**His decision: exempt the STEP, not the CONTROL.** His reasoning: the
plan-pitch size is a specific, deliberate, repeatedly-quoted ruling for that
exact control, and W16 is a general audit rule that predates it — the specific
instruction wins over the general one.

`.bk-planpitch` is untouched. **W16 is now satisfied with one documented
exception**, and `sweep-booking-steps.mjs` prints the exemption and the reason
every run instead of a failure:

    2/7 Add-ons   EXEMPT from W16 — over 476px, and .bk-planpitch is 823px of it

**The exemption is narrow three ways and cannot become an excuse:** it needs
`.bk-planpitch` on the page (0 everywhere else, so no other step can reach it);
it is arithmetic rather than a name, so everything else on the step is still
held to W16 to the pixel (without the pitch it fits by 465 / 342 / 498 / 347px);
and it stops firing the moment anything else grows — proved by sabotage, a
`min-height: 1200px` on `.bk-choices` made it vanish and the step reported
`over 1089px (121% of the screen)`. Steps 1 and 3 still fail in the same run.

**`sweep-widths.mjs` needed nothing** — checked, not assumed: it has no
vertical-fit check at all and the customer flow's add-ons step is not one of
its surfaces.

**The flow now reports 14 steps not fitting plus 3 exempt readings**, down from
25 at the start of the session.

## House rules this session learned the hard way

All are written up in `docs/gotchas.md`, which grew from 466 to ~1,040 lines
tonight. The ones that cost the most time:

- **A check that cannot run is not neutral** — it is a change budget nobody is
  counting against. The booking sweep was broken while 8.4 spent 264px.
- **A skip, an allowlist or a declared gap must be refusable**, or it becomes
  where defects go to be forgotten.
- **`settle()` is a cap on a quiet DOM, not a wait for a thing.** Six instances
  before tonight; two more tonight, one of them inside a guard written to make
  failures legible.
- **Two containers, not one.** `RecordHost` chooses between `.sheet` and
  `.col-2.record`; a selector naming one is right at half the widths and
  silently wrong at the others. This bit twice in one week.
- **A tint of the accent is a ground.** An "Added" badge nearly shipped at
  **3.14:1** for a tenant with a dark accent. `accent-sweep.mjs` measures the
  places that already existed and would not have caught it.
- **Measure before believing a word like "dim" or "faint."** Both complaints in
  8.4 were hierarchy, not contrast — the price measured 16.6:1.

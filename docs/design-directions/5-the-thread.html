Before you ask

Questions.

Do I need to already have a website?

No. A Facebook page and a phone number is the normal starting point.

I have a website, it's just old. Can you use it?

No, and you don't want me to. You get a new one — same business, same name, built for a phone. Send me the old one and I'll pull the photos and wording worth keeping.

Who owns the domain?

You do. It's in your name and it leaves with you.

How long until I'm taking bookings?

Same day. Name, hours, one service is enough.

What happens to my customers if I cancel?

You export them and go. The list was always yours.

Do you take a cut of my jobs?

No. A $600 coating costs you the same as a $65 wash.

Can I change my prices myself?

Yes, from your phone, and the site updates the second you save.

What if I have staff?

They get their own login and see only their jobs, not your money.

# Detailing Platform — Fix List

Site: https://detailingplatform.com
Source: a review of the live site done in a real browser on 2026-09-15 (Pacific). It covered the home page, pricing, examples, the full customer booking flow (stopped before sending a request), the detailer dashboard and the back office.

## Rules for whoever works this list

1. **Fix, don't rebuild.** The pages are well made. Only touch a screen for a problem named here. No redesigns.
2. **Work top to bottom.** The list is ordered by what costs money or trust first.
3. **Check every fix yourself in a real browser before marking it done.** Open the page and press the buttons. Try the case that should work *and* the case that should be refused. Check both a wide screen and a phone-width screen (375 wide). Don't mark anything done on the strength of a code change alone.
4. **Keep this file up to date.** Tick the box and add one line saying how you checked it, so nothing gets checked twice.
5. **Ask Andrew for sign-in details.** They are not in this file.
6. Where an item says **(not confirmed)**, look first. It was a strong guess, not something seen directly.

## Part 2 — Customer booking page (`/book/<slug>`)

- [ ] **2.1 Far-away addresses are accepted with no travel charge.**
  - **Seen:** on step 4 with "Long Beach" chosen, a San Diego address (1549 El Prado) went through to the time step with no warning and no "+$40 Beyond 15 miles" charge. The customer also picks their own area from a list, so they choose whether to pay the travel fee. The demo's "Where you work" setup is unfinished, which may be why.
  - **Fix:**
    - Work out distance from the looked-up address and apply the travel charge automatically. Remove the customer's area choice, or fill it in from the address.
    - Refuse addresses outside the service area: "We don't cover that area yet — call us at …"
    - Don't let a booking page take bookings until "Where you work" is set.
  - **Done when:**
    - A far address is refused.
    - A 15+ mile address adds the charge, and it shows in the summary.
    - A local address goes through with no charge.

- [ ] **2.2 The summary lines don't add up to the total.**
  - **Seen:** step 7 showed Wash & Wax $140, Interior Refresh $95, Pet hair $40, Vehicle size $60, Weekend rate $30, Member 10% off −$37. That sums to $328, but the total shows **$330**, "Rounded up to the nearest dollar."
  - **Also seen:** on step 2, adding the member rate took $275 to $245, when 10% off should give $247.50.
  - **Fix:** round each line once, and make the total exactly the sum of the lines shown. Remove the "rounded up" note.
  - **Done when:** the lines add to the total on every step, for several combinations (with and without member rate, weekend, vehicle size, add-ons).

- [ ] **2.3 The price jumps when a weekend day is picked, with no warning.**
  - **Seen:** the total went from $300 to $330 after picking Saturday. "Weekend rate" only appears on step 7.
  - **Fix:** mark weekend days in the calendar with "+$30", and add the line to the running total footer the moment a weekend day is picked.
  - **Done when:** the extra cost is visible before or at the moment the day is picked.

- [ ] **2.4 Continue is greyed out with no reason (step 3).**
  - **Seen:** with size and model filled in, Continue stayed disabled until a year was typed. The year box isn't marked required; the box above it is marked "(optional)."
  - **Fix:** make year optional. In general, never silently disable Continue: let it be pressed, then highlight the missing box with a plain message.
  - **Done when:** step 3 continues without a year. Any other required box, when missing, shows a message on press.

- [ ] **2.5 Continue is greyed out with no reason (step 6).**
  - **Seen:** the hint says "Add your name and phone number to continue." With both filled in, Continue stayed disabled until an email was added. It may only be required when a monthly plan is picked.
  - **Fix:** make the hint say exactly what's required in each case. If email is required for plans, say "Email needed for monthly plans." Otherwise accept phone only.
  - **Done when:** the hint always matches what's actually required, and pressing Continue with something missing says what.

- [ ] **2.6 The service area is asked too late.**
  - **Seen:** mobile address and area come at step 4, after services, add-ons and vehicle.
  - **Fix:** ask "Where's the car?" (mobile address or drop-off) before vehicle details, ideally at step 1 or 2. Keep the rest of the flow as is.
  - **Done when:** an out-of-area customer is told within the first two steps.

- [ ] **2.7 Monthly plans are confusing, and they sit on the add-ons step.**
  - **Seen:**
    - "Bi-weekly maintenance" is every 2 weeks, $120/month. "Monthly two-visit" is also 2 visits a month, but $200, tagged "2 visits each time."
    - Choosing "Member rate" never says whether joining costs anything or repeats.
  - **Fix:**
    - Move plans off the add-ons step into their own clearly labelled step, or a separate "Plans" page linked from the booking.
    - For each plan, show what it costs, how often it charges, and how to stop it.
    - Rename "Monthly two-visit" (e.g. "Monthly full care: 1 exterior + 1 interior") and remove the "2 visits each time" tag.
  - **Done when:** a customer can tell what a plan costs and how often it charges without guessing.

- [ ] **2.8 Spanish is half-translated.**
  - **Seen (ES):** "Weekend rate", "Member rate · 10% off", "Member rate aplicado", "That code isn't one of ours" stay in English.
  - **Also seen:** the language choice carried over to a different booking link (`/book/nope` showed its not-found message in Spanish).
  - **Fix:** translate every system line. Service names typed by the detailer can stay as typed. Keep the language choice per business, or default to English on a new business.
  - **Done when:** a full ES pass through all 7 steps shows no system-written English.

- [ ] **2.9 The last step says nothing about what happens next.**
  - **Seen:** "Request this time" with no word on when the detailer replies, deposits, or cancellation.
  - **Fix:** add one line above the button: "You'll get a text/email when [business] confirms, usually within X hours. Free to change or cancel up to X hours before."
  - **Done when:** the line shows, using the detailer's own booking rules.

- [ ] **2.10 The promo error is partly hidden behind the bottom bar.**
  - **Seen:** "That code isn't one of ours." sits under the sticky footer until you scroll.
  - **Fix:** scroll the error into view, or add bottom space so it is visible.
  - **Done when:** the error is fully visible on a phone without scrolling.

- [ ] **2.11 "What's included" is hidden behind an eye icon.**
  - **Seen:** the service list shows name and price only. The details open from an eye icon that doesn't read as "details."
  - **Fix:** use a text link, "What's included", in place of or next to the icon. Also remove the duplicate first bullet ("Everything in the Express Wash" appears both as the sentence and as bullet 1).
  - **Done when:** a first-time user can see how to open the details.

- [ ] **2.12 Selected services are hard to spot.**
  - **Seen:** the only sign of selection is a thin green border.
  - **Fix:** add a checkmark or "Added" label on selected cards.
  - **Done when:** selected and unselected cards are obvious at a glance on a phone.

- [ ] **4.9 Late jobs aren't flagged.**

Questions

Asked most weeks.

How long does a ceramic coating actually last?
The two-year and five-year figures are the manufacturer's warranty terms, and we register them in your name. Washed properly — pH-neutral, no automatic washes — they routinely outlast them. Washed badly, nothing lasts.
Do I need to leave the car with you?
For a correction, yes — two or three days, because the coating cures in the unit overnight and cannot be rushed. Maintenance washes and the gloss enhancement we can do at your place within about fifteen miles.
Can you quote from photos?
Roughly. The ladder on this page is the honest starting point; the exact number depends on paint condition, which we measure at drop-off. If it needs to change we say so before a pad touches the car, and you can walk away.
Is a ceramic coating the same as paint protection film?
No. A coating is a hard, thin layer that resists chemicals and UV and makes the car easier to keep clean; it will not stop a stone chip. Film is a physical layer that does. Many of our customers coat the whole car and film the front edge.
What if I need to move my slot?
The link in your confirmation email lets you change or cancel it yourself, up to 24 hours before. Inside 24 hours, call us — we can usually do something.

Questions

Asked every day

Do I need to book?
No, for a wash and vac — drive in. Yes for the interior deep and the full detail, because those take a bay for hours.
Is it a machine wash?
No. Two people, hand wash, hand dry. No brushes ever touch the paint.
Why is my price different from the menu?
It should not be. The only thing that changes it is size, and the two sizes are on the menu. If the interior needs more than a vacuum we tell you before we start.
Do you get pet hair out?
Most of it, for $25. Heavily embedded hair in old cloth sometimes needs the interior deep instead, and we will say so rather than take the $25 and disappoint you.
What if it rains?
We stay open. A wash in the rain is still a clean car, and we do not charge you again if it is wet by the time you get home.

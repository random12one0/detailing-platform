Questions

The ones we get every week

Why does the price change after you look at it?
On most cars it does not — the rate card is the rate card. It changes when there is something the ladder cannot see: a third row, heavy pet hair, smoke, or paint that needs two passes instead of one. We tell you on the phone, before the work, every time.
Do you get all the pet hair out?
Nearly always. Embedded hair in older cloth sometimes needs a second pass, which we charge at half. We will tell you which one you have when we see it.
How often should I have it done?
Twice a year for most people in Iowa, and once more after a salted winter. If you park outside under trees, three times.
What if it needs more than the package I booked?
We ring you. We do not add work and invoice for it afterwards — that is the fastest way to lose somebody who has been coming for a decade.
Do you come to me?
No. We are a shop with three bays, because bays let us do coatings and correction properly. We will collect the car within twelve miles for $35 and bring it back.
Is the coating a wax?
No. A wax lasts a few months; the three-year coating is a bonded layer that survives washing. It is also not a force field — it will not stop a stone chip.

Questions

The ones that matter on an old car

Will you machine-polish original paint?

Only after a depth reading and a conversation, and usually we will advise against it. Single-stage lacquer is thin, fifty years of it is thinner, and a car only has original paint once. Hand glazing gets most of the way with none of the risk.

Can you get the pitting out of the chrome?

No. Pitting is metal that has gone. We can make the rest of it bright and seal it so it stops getting worse; anything more is re-plating, and we will give you two people who do that properly.

Is a ceramic coating a good idea?

On a modern repaint, sometimes. On original paint, no — it is a decision that is hard to undo and it changes how the car looks under judging. We would rather wax it four times a year.

Why is it priced by the day?

Because we cannot know from a photograph whether the wood needs an hour or a week. A package price on a 1963 car is a guess with a number attached, and one of us ends up unhappy.

Do you come to the car?

Yes, for detailing work. Anything needing a controlled environment we collect on an enclosed trailer.

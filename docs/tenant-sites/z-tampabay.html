Before you book

Before you book.

The deposit

$25 holds a slot and comes off the final bill. It is not refundable. A slot cancelled late cannot be refilled, and somebody on the waiting list loses their week too.

If we cannot do the job when we arrive

You are charged the full amount of the booked service. That includes nobody being there to let us in. Reschedule instead — a phone call the day before costs you nothing.

Hazards in the vehicle

Needles, fuel, solvents, loose chemicals, anything sharp: tell us before the appointment, not when we open the door. We will still take the job. You are responsible for anything we are not warned about.

Weather

We call and move the booking for rain or for heat over about 95°F. Next open day, and the deposit follows the booking.

We do not rush a job

If a detail needs six hours it gets six hours. If the vehicle turns out to need more than we quoted, you are told during the work, not on the invoice.

Water and power

The van carries both. You do not need an outside tap, an outlet, or to be home — just somewhere legal to park it.

Common questions.

How long does a Full Detail take?

About four hours for a sedan, five for an SUV. We book one job per slot, so nobody is rushed and we are not juggling two driveways.

What happens if it rains?

Light rain is fine. Heavy rain or under 4°C and we move you free to the next slot that suits. You will hear from us the night before.

Where do you need to park?

Any flat spot with roughly a car’s width beside it — a driveway, a kerb or an office car park all work.

How late can I cancel?

Free up to 24 hours before, from the link in your confirmation. Inside 24 hours it is 50%, because the slot cannot be refilled.

Can you get scratches out?

Light swirls and haze, yes — that is what the polish stage is for. Deeper scratches you can catch a fingernail in need the Ceramic Package’s correction, and a few need a body shop. Send photos and we will tell you which.

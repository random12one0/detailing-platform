Questions

The ones we actually get

Why does the price change after you look at it?

Because condition is most of the work. A three-year-old car that lives in a garage and a three-year-old car that carries a dog to the beach are the same vehicle and not the same job. The ladder above is where we start; we confirm before we touch anything.

Do you need my water and power?

No. We carry 60 gallons and our own generator. If you have an outside tap we will use it and take $10 off, but nothing depends on it.

What happens if it rains?

Interior work goes ahead. Exterior work moves to the next dry slot and we text you the night before — you are never charged for a move we made.

Do you get all the pet hair out?

Almost always, and we will tell you honestly if we cannot. Heavily embedded hair in older cloth sometimes needs a second visit, which we price at half.

How often should I have it done?

On the coast, every eight weeks keeps salt out of the paint. Inland, twice a year is plenty. We would rather you booked less often than felt sold to.

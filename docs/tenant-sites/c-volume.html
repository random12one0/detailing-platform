Questions

How long will you have the car?

The board says how long each job takes. Drop-offs go into the queue in order, so a 7:30 drop for a full detail is ready by lunch; an afternoon drop is ready the next morning.

Which size is my car?

Small is a coupe, sedan or hatchback. Medium is a crossover, small SUV or wagon. Large is a truck, three-row SUV or van. If you're on the line, we'll go with the cheaper one.

Can the price change after I book?

Only for the things the board marks "priced from", and only after we've looked at the car with you. Nothing is added after the work starts.

Do you do ceramic coating?

Not on the board. Our sealants last a season; a coating is a two-day job we quote separately. Ask at the counter and we'll tell you honestly whether your paint is ready for one.

What if I need to cancel?

Cancel or move it from the link in your confirmation email. Up to 24 hours before, no charge. Same-day, we ask that you call so the slot can go to the next car in the queue.

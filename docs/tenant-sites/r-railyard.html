Terms & common questionsUpdated 09/2026
Do we need to supply water or power?
No. We arrive with a 900-gallon bowser and a silent generator. If you have a standpipe we will use it and take 4% off the band.
What happens to the runoff?
Bunded mats under every unit, tankered off site, disposal certificates issued monthly with the invoice. This is usually the question that decides the contract.
What if a unit is not on the lot?
We photograph the empty bay and it is simply not invoiced. We do not charge for units we did not clean.
Why does a rate change mid-contract?
Only if your volume band changes, and only in your favour within the month. A band increase applies from the following month and we email before it does.
Do you clean interiors?
Cab wipe-down is in every rate. A full cab deep clean is an add-on. Trailer interiors are a separate line because they are a different job.

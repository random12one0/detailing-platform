Before you book

Questions we get asked.

Why does the price change after you look at the car?

The listed price assumes normal use. Heavy swirls, etched water spots or a car that has never been corrected take more stages, and we tell you the new number before we start, not after.

Will every scratch come out?

No. If a scratch catches your fingernail it is through the clear coat and polishing will not remove it. We read the paint depth first and tell you which ones are staying.

Do you remove all stains and pet hair?

Most of it. Heavy pet hair and set-in stains are charged as an add-on because they can double the interior time.

Do you need power and water?

No. The van carries both. We need a parking space beside the car and about eight feet of room to work.

How often should I have this done?

A correction is a one-off. After it, a maintenance wash every four to six weeks keeps the finish; a coating stretches that out.

What if my car needs more than the package I booked?

We call you before touching it. Nothing is charged at booking, and you can cancel free up to 24 hours before.

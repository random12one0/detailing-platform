Before you book

The awkward questions.

Why at night?

Panel temperature. Compound flashes off too fast on hot paint and a coating will not level, so from May to October the work starts after the sun is off the car.

How long will it take?

Four hours for a one stage, up to nine for a two stage, and a coating adds a day of curing you cannot drive in rain.

What if it rains?

We move you, free, to the next dry evening. A coating that gets wet inside 24 hours has to be redone.

Will every scratch come out?

No. If a scratch catches your fingernail it is through the clear coat and polishing will not remove it. We read the paint depth first and tell you which ones are staying.

Where do you park?

A flat driveway with about eight feet round the car. No garage needed — the lights are ours.

Can I cancel?

Free up to 24 hours before. Nothing is charged when you book.

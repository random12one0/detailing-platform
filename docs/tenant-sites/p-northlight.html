Questions

The ones we actually get

Do you need my water and power?

No. We carry both. If you would rather we used your outside tap we will, and it takes $10 off.

What if it rains? It is Portland.

We work under a canopy. Interior always goes ahead; heavy correction work we will move rather than rush, and you are never charged for a move we made.

Why does the price change after you look at it?

Condition is most of the work. The ladder above assumes a normally used car. We tell you the number on the drive, before anything is wet.

Where can you park?

A driveway, a wide kerb, or a flat spot in a car park. We need about two car lengths and we cannot work on a hill.

Do you take the car away?

No. Everything happens where the car is, which is the point.

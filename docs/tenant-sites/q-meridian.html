Questions

Before you arrange

Why is it more than a mobile detailer?

Because the car is with us for days rather than hours, in filtered light where defects are visible, and because we do one car at a time. If what you want is a very good wash, a mobile detailer is the right call and we will say so on the phone.

Why does the price change after you see it?

We take a paint-depth reading before any machine work. A car that has been polished before may not have the clear coat for a two-stage.

How long will you have it?

Two days for a studio detail, four for correction and coating. We agree the return date before collection and we do not move it.

Is it insured while you have it?

Yes — $5m, in transit and on site. The certificate is on the wall and we will email a copy before collection.

Do you take classics?

Yes, and single-stage paint is quoted separately because it polishes differently. Enclosed transport by arrangement.

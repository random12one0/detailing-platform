What we cannot fix, and will tell you before taking your money

  • Burns and tears. Cleaning does not close a hole. We can put you on to a trim shop.
  • Long-term smoke in headlining. We improve it. On a car smoked in for years we do not remove it, and anyone promising otherwise is guessing.
  • Dye transfer from jeans on light leather. Sometimes. Often not. We will test a patch and show you before we commit.
  • A leak. If water is still getting in, the smell comes back in a fortnight. Fix the leak first — we would rather lose the booking.

Questions

Asked every week

Will the smell definitely go?

If the source is gone, usually yes. If the source is still there — a leak, a damp carpet under a mat, something in a door card — it comes back, and we would rather find that on the day than take $450 from you twice.

Why does the price change after you see it?

Because “a bit of dog hair” and what we sometimes find are different jobs by a factor of three. We look, we tell you the number, and you can say no with nothing to pay.

Do you do the outside as well?

No. Never have. There are three very good people in the Twin Cities who do paint and we will happily give you their numbers.

How long does it take to dry?

Two to four hours with the windows down, longer in winter. We leave absorbent packs in and text you when it is safe to shut it up.

Do you need my drive?

A flat spot and about ten feet around the car. We carry water and power.

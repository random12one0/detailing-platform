The warranty

Five years, and here is exactly what voids it

Written out before you book rather than handed over afterwards. If any of this is a problem, it is better that you know now.

  1. An annual inspection, within thirty days of the anniversary. Booked here, takes an hour, and it is free for the life of the warranty. Miss the window and the coating is still on the car — the warranty is not.
  2. No automatic car washes. Not the touchless ones either. Hand wash, two buckets, pH-neutral soap; I will show you and give you the first bottle.
  3. Damage is not covered, and neither is wear. Stone chips, scratches, kerbed wheels and clear-coat failure that was already there are outside it. What IS covered is the coating losing its hydrophobic behaviour or its gloss before the five years are up.
  4. It is on the car, not on you. The warranty transfers with the vehicle if you sell it. Tell me and I will reissue the certificate in the new name.

The certificate is a PDF and a card for the glovebox, both issued the day you collect. Terms are Gtechniq's; I am an accredited installer and the claim goes through me.

Questions

Before you book

Will I be able to see the edges?
On the bonnet, no — we wrap the leading edge. On wings and bumpers there is a cut line where the panel ends, and on a light colour you will find it if you look for it. Anyone telling you it is completely invisible has not fitted much film.
Does it need correcting first?
Usually a little. Film locks in whatever is underneath, permanently. We inspect and tell you honestly — and since we do not sell correction, we have no reason to over-recommend it.
How long does it last?
Ten-year warranty on the film against yellowing and cracking. Real life on a daily driver in Texas sun is about seven to nine years on horizontal panels.
Can I wash it normally?
After seven days, yes. No pressure washer within a foot of an edge, ever.
What if a panel gets damaged?
We replace that panel's film at cost plus fitting. That is why we price by panel — so one incident does not mean redoing the whole car.

05

Questions

The seven a first-time customer actually asks, answered with the number or the step rather than with a paragraph.

What does detailing actually mean?

Cleaning and correcting a car surface by surface rather than panel by panel. A wash removes what is sitting on the paint; a detail removes what has bonded to it and then puts a layer back on top.

What is in a full detail?

Decontamination, one machine polishing pass, a sealant, and an interior that includes the trunk floor and the spare tire well. The full list for the Enhancement package is above.

How often should a car be detailed?

A maintenance visit every three to four months holds a sealed car. A full enhancement once a year, or once after a winter that had salt on the roads.

What does it cost?

$620 to $1,645 depending on the package and the vehicle size, before inspection. Restoration work starts at $10,000 and is quoted in person because the number depends on how much clear coat is left.

What steps does a detailer take?

Wash, decontaminate, dry, correct, protect — in that order. Skipping decontamination means grinding whatever is bonded to the paint into it with the polisher.

What products go on the car?

pH-neutral shampoo, an iron remover, a fine polishing compound, and a silica spray sealant. Coating work uses manufacturer-certified film and a nine-hour cure.

How do you tell a good detailer from a bad one?

Ask what light they inspect under and how many polishing passes the price covers. A quote that does not mention either is a wash with a longer name.

Who's turning up

It's just me. That's the point.

I'm Sam. I started washing cars in my parents' driveway in Fife the summer I was sixteen, mostly to pay for the car I was washing. Six years and one very tired van later, it's the whole job. I don't have a crew, I don't subcontract, and I'm not going to send someone you've never met to your house. If you book me, I'm the one who shows up.

The van carries its own water and power, so I can work anywhere I can park — a driveway, an apartment lot, the street outside your office. I use pH-neutral soap, two buckets, and a proper extractor for interiors rather than a shop vac and hope. It takes longer than a drive-through. That's the difference you're paying for.

If I wouldn't hand the keys back to my mum, I'm not done yet.

Most of my week is regulars — people I've seen every four or six weeks for years. I'd like you to be one of them, but I'd rather you book once, see the car, and decide. Text me a photo if you're not sure what it needs. I'll tell you honestly, including when the cheaper option is the right one.

— Sam Tolliver

Before you ask

The things people text me.

If yours isn't here, the number's at the top.

Do I need to be home?
No. Leave it unlocked or leave a key somewhere we've agreed, and I'll text you when I'm done. Most of my regulars are at work.
Do you need my water or power?
No. The van has a 400-litre tank and a generator. If you'd rather I used your tap to save me a refill, tell me on the day — it's not required.
What if it's raining?
Interiors go ahead regardless. For a wash or a polish I'll move you to the next dry day I have, and I'll text the evening before rather than the morning of.
Apartment lots, offices, the street?
All fine as long as I can park the van within a hose-length of the car and I'm not going to flood somebody's doorway. Some managed lots ask for notice — check with your building first.
How far do you go?
About twenty miles from Tacoma. Puyallup, Lakewood, University Place, Gig Harbor and Federal Way are all normal. Beyond that I'll come for a full detail or a plan, not for a wash on its own.

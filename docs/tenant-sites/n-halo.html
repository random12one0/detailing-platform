Questions

Before you ask

Why does the price change after you look at it?

Because we measure the clear coat before we cut into it. A car that has been machine-polished before may not have the depth for a two-step, and we will tell you that rather than take the money.

Will a coating stop stone chips?

No. That is paint protection film, which is a different product at a different price. A coating makes the car easier to wash and keeps the gloss; it is a few microns thick.

Can you coat at my house?

No, and anyone who says yes is guessing. Dust settles into a curing coating and there is no fixing it afterwards without polishing it off.

How long am I without the car?

Two days for Halo 3, three for Halo 5. We have two loan cars, first come first served, free.

What happens if it rains in the first week?

Rain is fine. Washing is not — no pressure washer, no automatic wash, no soap for seven days. We send the aftercare by text.

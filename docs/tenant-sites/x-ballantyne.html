The process

Inspect, wash, correct, protect

STAGE 1

Inspect

A walk round in daylight and under a hand-held lamp. Defects noted, package agreed, price fixed before anything is touched.

STAGE 2

Wash and decontaminate

Two-bucket hand wash, iron fallout remover, clay bar. Polishing dirty paint grinds the dirt into it.

STAGE 3

Correct

Machine polish to the agreed stage. One pass for light swirls, two for paint that has been through tunnel washes.

STAGE 4

Protect

Wax, sealant or coating. A coating is wiped, levelled and left to cure — which is why it is booked on a dry day.

Before you book

Common questions

How long does it take?

Two to four hours for an interior, three to five for a full detail, six to nine when a coating goes on. You get the window when you book, not on the day.

Do I need to be there?

No. Most people leave the car unlocked with the key inside and pay by card when they get the photos.

What do you need from me?

A parking space about a car and a half long. Water and power come off the van.

What happens if it rains?

A wash or an interior still runs. A coating does not — it needs twelve dry hours to cure, so it gets moved, free.

Is a sealant the same as a ceramic coating?

No. A sealant lasts four to six months. A coating is rated three to five years and needs the paint corrected first, which is most of the cost.

Will it take the scratches out?

Anything that does not catch a fingernail usually comes out. Deeper than that is filled and improved, and you are told which is which before the polisher starts.

Do you do engine bays and headlights?

Both, as add-ons. Headlight restoration is one visit; a yellowed lens goes clear and is sealed against it coming back.

Pet hair, smoke, spilled milk?

All doable and all charged as add-ons, because each of them is an hour or two on its own.

Why does the price change with the car?

Three sizes. A coupe, an SUV and a three-row truck are not the same amount of paint or the same number of seats.

What if the car is worse than it looked?

You get a photo and a number before anything starts. Say no and only the booked package runs.

When do I pay?

When the job is finished and you have walked round it. Card, tap or cash.

Can I cancel?

Free up to 24 hours before. Inside that, half the package price, because the slot cannot be refilled.
